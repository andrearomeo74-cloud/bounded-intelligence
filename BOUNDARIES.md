# Boundaries and Scope

This repository defines a **theoretical framework** for *Limited Intelligence* and systemic coherence under proportional constraints.

It exists to clarify **where intelligence holds** and **where it necessarily breaks** when proportionality is violated.

## What This Repository Explicitly DOES

- Defines **conceptual boundaries** for intelligence expansion
- Frames intelligence as a balance between:
  - **E** – Energy (cost, compute, metabolic or systemic load)
  - **I** – Information (throughput, noise, uncertainty)
  - **S** – Structure (complexity, coupling, fragility)
  - **V** – Functional Value (capability, outcome value, legitimacy)
- Provides a **governance-oriented lens** for AI, biological, and complex systems
- Supports discussion, critique, and research framing
- Remains **non-empirical, non-clinical, non-operational**

## What This Repository Explicitly DOES NOT Do

- ❌ It is **not** an implementation of any AI, AGI, or decision-making system  
- ❌ It does **not** provide algorithms, thresholds, optimization rules, or control logic  
- ❌ It does **not** claim predictive, clinical, medical, or therapeutic validity  
- ❌ It does **not** propose enforcement mechanisms or policy instruments  
- ❌ It does **not** define consensus rules or moral authority  

No amount of optimization in **E**, **I**, or **S** is claimed to restore coherence once **V (value legitimacy)** is fractured.

## On Claims and Limitations

- All formulations are **conceptual and comparative**
- Mathematical expressions are **symbolic**, not quantitative models
- Any application beyond theory requires independent validation and domain-specific rigor

## Governance Principle

This framework treats **limits not as failures**, but as **structural safeguards**.

Coherence emerges only under **bounded growth**.  
When proportionality is exceeded, efficiency degrades, signals dilute, and reasoning fractures into noise.

## Status

This repository is intentionally bounded.  
Expansion is permitted **only if proportionality, clarity, and legitimacy are preserved**.

---

*Boundaries are not constraints on intelligence —  
they are the conditions that make intelligence possible.*
