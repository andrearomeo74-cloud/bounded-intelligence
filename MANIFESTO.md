# Manifesto for Limited Intelligence

## Why Limits Matter

Intelligence does not fail because it is limited.  
It fails when it grows without proportion.

This framework starts from a simple premise:  
**coherence is not maximized by scale, but preserved by proportion**.

Limits are not obstacles to intelligence.  
They are the conditions that make intelligence *legible, accountable, and legitimate*.

---

## Against Unbounded Expansion

Across domains — artificial intelligence, biological systems, complex organizations — breakdown does not originate from insufficient capability, but from **asymmetry**:

- energy growing faster than value,
- information exceeding interpretability,
- structure expanding beyond stability,
- capability outpacing consequence.

When proportional constraints are violated, optimization no longer improves intelligence — it degrades it.
Signal turns into noise.
Efficiency collapses.
Purpose erodes.

Unlimited intelligence is not superintelligence.
It is incoherent intelligence.

---

## Intelligence as a Bounded System

This repository treats intelligence as a **bounded system**, governed by four interdependent dimensions:

- **E — Energy** (cost, compute, metabolic or systemic load)
- **I — Information** (throughput, noise, uncertainty)
- **S — Structure** (complexity, coupling, fragility)
- **V — Functional Value** (capability, outcome value, legitimacy)

Coherence exists only when these dimensions remain proportionate.
Growth is valid only while proportionality holds.

Limits are therefore not failures, but **structural guarantees**.

---

## Governance Before Optimization

This framework is not about maximizing performance.  
It is about preserving **legitimacy**.

In any intelligent system, once value (V) fractures, no amount of optimization in energy, information, or structure can restore coherence.
Alignment cannot be enforced after the fact.
It must emerge from proportional constraints.

For this reason, *Congruity / Limited Intelligence* is a **governance principle**, not a consensus mechanism and not a control system.

---

## What This Is — and Is Not

This repository provides:
- a **theoretical and comparative framework**
- a **public, readable conceptual scaffold**
- a **lens for reasoning about limits, alignment, and reliability**
- a foundation for discussion across AI, biology, and complex systems

This repository does **not** provide:
- implementations
- algorithms
- thresholds or decision rules
- clinical, medical, or therapeutic claims
- predictive or operational models

All formulations are **conceptual and non-empirical by design**.

---

## A Final Constraint

If intelligence is allowed to grow without accounting for its costs, consequences, and structural limits,
it does not become free —
it becomes unstable.

Bounded intelligence is not weaker intelligence.

It is the only form that remains coherent.
