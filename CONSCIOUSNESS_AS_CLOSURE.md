# Consciousness as Informational Closure

## Scope

This document does not propose a physical theory of consciousness,
nor a neuroscientific or quantum-mechanical mechanism.

Instead, it introduces a **structural framing**:
consciousness is treated as a **primary domain of informational closure**,
not as an emergent byproduct of energy, computation, or complexity.

---

## Inversion of the standard assumption

Mainstream approaches typically assume:
matter → energy → computation → cognition → consciousness.

Within the Congruity framework, this chain is inverted:

closure → experience → information → structure → energy cost

Energy and computation are **downstream costs**, not generative primitives.

---

## Consciousness as admissibility condition

A system is conscious not because it processes information,
but because information **closes locally** into a self-consistent experiential domain.

Key properties:
- information is internally coherent,
- meaning is intrinsic, not observer-assigned,
- experience is irreducible to external description.

Consciousness is therefore not something systems *produce*,
but something systems *enter* when closure conditions are met.

---

## Relation to quantum descriptions

Quantum formalisms become relevant not because consciousness is “quantum”,
but because classical descriptions fail to preserve closure at minimal scales.

Indeterminacy, non-local correlation, and observer participation
signal limits of classical grammar — not mystical properties.

Quantum theory is thus interpreted as a **boundary formalism**:
it marks where informational closure cannot be externally factorized.

---

## Alignment with Congruity 7.0

Within Congruity:

- Energy = cost of unresolved structure,
- Information = constraint on coherence,
- Structure = carrier of closure,
- Value = persistence of meaning.

Consciousness corresponds to **closure at the level of value**,
where experience remains internally admissible without external reference.

This framing is compatible with — but not dependent on —
panpsychism, emergence, or computationalism.
It operates at a **meta-theoretical level**.

---

## What this framing avoids

- No claim that consciousness violates physics,
- No collapse interpretation or causal intervention,
- No claim that intelligence implies consciousness.

The goal is **structural clarity**, not explanation by mechanism.
