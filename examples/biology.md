# Biology & Natural Systems

## 1. Minimal Nervous Systems (Bounded Decisions)

**Claim:** Very small biological systems can exhibit highly efficient and reliable behavior precisely because their intelligence is *structurally limited*.

**Conceptual mapping**
- **V (Value):** survival-relevant behavior (navigation, feeding, avoidance)
- **E (Energy):** metabolic and physiological cost
- **I (Information):** sensory bandwidth and uncertainty
- **S (Structure):** circuit complexity and fragility

**Congruity reading**
When energy and information are tightly bounded, organisms rely on simple, robust heuristics.  
High functional value is achieved with low total burden (**E + I + S**), yielding high coherence (**C**).

---

## 2. Evolution of Bounded Rationality

**Claim:** In uncertain environments, simple heuristics often outperform complex optimization strategies when cognitive and energetic costs are taken into account.

**Congruity reading**
Increasing information processing or structural complexity does not guarantee proportional gains in value.  
Bounded strategies preserve coherence by preventing over-scaling relative to evolutionary payoff.
