# Positioning & Scope — Limited Intelligence Framework

## What this repository is about

This repository presents **Limited Intelligence** as a *theoretical and governance-oriented framework*.

“Limited” does **not** mean weak, deficient, or incomplete.  
It means **bounded, proportional, and structurally constrained by design**.

The core assumption is that **coherence, reliability, and legitimacy emerge only under bounded growth**.  
When capability scales faster than its energetic, informational, or structural costs, systems lose coherence.

This framework treats limits as **guarantees**, not restrictions.

---

## Core principle

> **Capability ∝ Cost ∝ Scale ∝ Consequence**

No asymmetry is left unaccounted.  
No exception is unpriced.

Intelligence remains coherent only when:
- energy (E),
- information (I),
- structure (S),
- and functional value (V)

remain **proportionally aligned**.

---

## What “Limited Intelligence” means

Limited Intelligence is intelligence that:
- scales **within proportional constraints**
- preserves **signal integrity** under growth
- maintains **traceability and accountability**
- remains **interpretable and governable**
- avoids optimization paths that generate hidden externalities

Limits are not imposed *after* the fact —  
they are **embedded at the structural level**.

---

## What this repository is NOT

This repository is **not**:

- an implementation of an AI system
- a training recipe or scaling strategy
- a performance optimization framework
- a medical, clinical, or therapeutic guide
- a release of proprietary algorithms, thresholds, or decision rules
- a claim about AGI realization or timelines

All formulations are:
- **theoretical**
- **conceptual**
- **comparative**
- **non-empirical**
- **non-clinical**

---

## Why this matters (AI, biology, complex systems)

Across domains, failure rarely originates from insufficient capability.  
It originates from **disproportion**.

Examples include:
- hallucinations in over-scaled AI systems
- fragility in highly optimized infrastructures
- metabolic or cognitive collapse in biological systems
- governance breakdown in unbounded socio-technical systems

In all cases, **unbounded intelligence increases risk faster than value**.

Limited Intelligence functions as a **structural firewall**.

---

## Governance perspective

From a governance standpoint:

- Limits preserve **coherence under scaling**
- Limits enable **accountability and auditability**
- Limits reduce systemic risk
- Limits make intelligence **legitimate**, not merely powerful

Unlimited intelligence is ungovernable by definition.  
Bounded intelligence is governable by design.
